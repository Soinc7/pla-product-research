Grading rules
  • Yellow → Orange: all 5 Meta keywords show no competitor using its Kalo top-10 videos.
  • Orange → Green: winning video proven — 500+ orders, 0.6+ sales per 1K views, 300K+ views, plus a 100K+ appeal video.
  • Est. delivered = 1688 item cost + $5.00 standard (shipping + fees) until a real supplier-agent quote replaces it.
  • Sales per 1K views is a TikTok conversion signal, not ROAS.
  • Launch math: margin = TikTok price − est. delivered · break-even ROAS = price ÷ margin · sales/day = $150 ÷ margin (rounded up) · 2.0-ROAS price = 2 × est. delivered · Kalo orders/month ≈ 30-day revenue ÷ price. Uses TikTok price as reference; your store price/bundles change this.
