## What this run showed about the skill

- **The Meta check changed the picture.** On cost alone, #7 Outlet spacers looked like the best product ($7.00). Meta shows it is saturated, with cloned ads and footage already in use. Without this check, it would have been picked first.
- **Footage reuse is real.** Two of the products checked (#1 and #7) have a top Kalo video that is likely already running as someone's Meta ad.
- **The limits to plan for:** TikTok rate-limits its embed players (compare thumbnails, or use Kalo's download), and loose keyword searches pull big-retailer catalog ads (use exact phrase).
